## Notes on figure quality
- All figures were extracted from the scanned PDF of K&N provided in this repository. Some figures sit at page edges or span page folds in the original scan; where a small label is clipped at a margin, the caption, figure number, and all pedagogically essential labels remain intact, and the notes provide the surrounding context in text + equations so the figure is an illustration, not the sole carrier of information. No figure has been redrawn, simplified, or synthesized — all crops are from K&N.
- Fig 9.5 (semi-log extraction of Δi″, Δi′) is not placed — the existing crop captures only a corner of the plot and the surrounding prose in §2.3 fully explains the concept.
- Fig 12.25 (equal-area for sudden line switching without a fault) is not placed because it illustrates the same equal-area idea as the placed Fig 12.26 (fault + line trip), which is the more important case for a first-time reader.
- All other unused figures (fig12_22 cropping edges aside) are either redundant with placed figures or illustrate advanced cases beyond first-pass scope.
- Figs. 10.13 and 10.14 (negative- and zero-sequence networks of a synchronous machine) are topologically identical to Fig. 10.12b except for the value of Z and (for zero) the 3Z<sub>n</sub> term; they were deliberately not cropped to avoid near-duplicate figures and are described in words + equations in 02 §1.11.
- Saadat and B&V were not used as figure sources (Saadat's PDF is image-only without a clean text layer; B&V was used only for cross-checks of intuition).
